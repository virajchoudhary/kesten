## Interface decisions

The terminal keeps the chart, instrument controls, private watchlist, and integrated research dock as its main working surfaces. Inter remains the interface family; IBM Plex Mono distinguishes prices, dates, symbols, and exact identities. The user-selected measured-frame symbol replaces both rejected K treatments. Neutral surfaces and restrained blue focus accents work in both themes; market direction keeps its existing semantic colors.

Two research arrangements were compared in source and rendered review. A fixed-height panel provides predictable dimensions but can consume the chart on shorter desktop viewports. The selected arrangement limits the panel by viewport height, keeps keyboard and pointer resizing, and preserves explicit maximize/restore controls. On phones the research surface remains a separate working area rather than compressing two charts into one screen.

Dataset and horizon selection precede the expandable source/permissions record. Saved evaluations retain a context column, model reference, replay/export controls, and exact identity disclosure. Compact chart-view controls use understated tabs; standalone research retains its existing selected-button treatment. No model, metric, dataset, cutoff, or result identity changes.

Chart study readouts now follow the inspected session for SMA, EMA, Bollinger bands, and RSI. Their legend states raw price currency separately from percentage or indexed price-scale settings. Missing warm-up values remain unavailable. A contained horizontal legend prevents long study values from wrapping over the plot; it is keyboard focusable. Existing chart types, scales, linked comparison, measurements, levels, layout persistence, volume, and library attribution remain.

The private watchlist gains saved/alphabetical/company ordering and keyboard symbol switching. Filtering and sorting share one visible list, so keyboard movement follows the order shown. Research actions open the native dock with the selected instrument while global navigation retains standalone research access. Other instruments' prices are not inferred from the selected instrument's snapshot.
